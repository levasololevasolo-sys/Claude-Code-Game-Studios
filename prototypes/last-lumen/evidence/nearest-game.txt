Nearest game: Dead Cells; this one differs in its hook: the room is dark and lit only by the knight's lantern, every landed hit charges that lantern (widening the light), and a full lantern is spent on a Flare that stuns, burns and lights the room, with boons that rewrite the Flare instead of handing out new weapons. Own setting (a drowned clockwork city, a moth-winged lantern knight, a bell-shaped boss), own figures and UI.
