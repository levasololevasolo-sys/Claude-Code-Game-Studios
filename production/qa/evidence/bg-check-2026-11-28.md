# Living level backgrounds and caves (2026-11-28)

Request (the developer): the background must change as the hero moves along the
level instead of standing still; underground sections need their own texture;
do it for every location.

## What changed

- `src/world/level_backdrop.gd` (new): builds the backgrounds of a graph level from
  `assets/data/level_backdrop.json`.
  - Surface horizon follows the floor of the surface room the camera is over
    (it used to be fixed at the entry room's floor, so other rooms showed empty sky).
  - `src/world/landmark_painter.gd` (new): a non-repeating layer of far
    landmarks across the whole level width (Топь: stilt huts, dead oaks, idols,
    broken bridges, grave posts, sunken towers; Лес: giant pines, watchtowers,
    stone circles; Мороз: ice spires, frozen idols). Never the same kind twice in a row.
  - Far layers drift toward `progress_tint` as the hero nears the level's end.
  - Underground rooms: `assets/shaders/cave_backdrop.gdshader` (far wall with
    parallax, boulders, stalactites, roots in the upper band, glowing dots,
    thin Navi cracks in the deep band). Replaces the flat dark back walls.
- Solid earth: `assets/shaders/earth_fill.gdshader` (mottling, strata, pebbles, roots).
- `src/world/backdrop.gd`: `tex_list` (several images per layer, laid without
  repeats, for the AI art), bottom caps (`tex_down`) so a painted layer's edge never shows.
- Forest and Frost now have graph levels with underground rooms
  (`room_templates_forest.json`, `room_templates_frost.json`).
- Art drop-in guide: `assets/art/backgrounds/README.md`.

## Evidence

`bg-<tier>-surface.png` (4 surface spots from the start to the far end) and
`bg-<tier>-under.png` (4 underground spots), tiers 1-3; `*-before.png` are the
same check on the previous code. Driver: `tests/integration/visual/bg_check_driver.gd`
(`-- --bg-check=<dir> --save=user://bg_save.json --fresh`).

## Checks

- gdUnit: 630 tests, 0 failures (9 new in tests/unit/level_gen/level_backdrop_test.gd).
- level2-check: 0 failed checks. run-check: 0 failed checks.
- perf-check (cloud, no GPU): CPU per frame without rendering 8.0 ms (was 7 ms);
  Forward+ on the software GPU 150 ms per frame (was 130 ms), 408 draw calls (was
  250-340). The extra cost is the cave shader's fill on a software rasteriser; it
  is a few simple noise lookups per pixel and is not expected to matter on an RTX 3050,
  but this was not measured on real hardware.
